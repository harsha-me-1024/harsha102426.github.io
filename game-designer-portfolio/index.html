About Me

Hey there, I'm Harsha! I'm a Game Designer who truly loves bringing imaginary worlds to life. For me, there's nothing quite like the feeling of sculpting an engaging level or designing a core game mechanic that finally "clicks" and feels incredibly satisfying to play.

I thrive on turning creative ideas into tangible systems. Whether I'm sketching out a detailed Game Design Document or relentlessly iterating to get a level layout just right, my goal is always the same: creating unforgettable experiences for the player.
