About Me

Hey there, I'm Harsha! I'm a Game Designer who truly loves bringing imaginary worlds to life. For me, there's nothing quite like the feeling of sculpting an engaging level or designing a core game mechanic that finally "clicks" and feels incredibly satisfying to play.

I thrive on turning creative ideas into tangible systems. Whether I'm sketching out a detailed Game Design Document or relentlessly iterating to get a level layout just right, my goal is always the same: creating unforgettable experiences for the player.

Design Philosophy & Practice

As a Game Designer & Level Designer, I bridge the gap between creative vision and executable mechanics. I focus on sculpting level pacing, verticality, movement feel, and gameplay flow that keeps players immersed.

What I Do

  • Level & Spatial Design: Crafting intuitive environments, line-of-sight cues, combat encounter blockouts, and verticality flow.
  • Mechanics & Systems Tuning: Balancing player progression loops, movement dynamics, and game economy values.
  • GDD Specification: Writing comprehensive Game Design Documents, logic flow diagrams, and feature specs ready for programming teams.
